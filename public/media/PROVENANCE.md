# Field photograph and video provenance

The public site shows twelve field photographs in the "From the field" gallery and one short drone video behind the opening headline. The site was redesigned on 11 November 2026: five photographs and the video were added, and the earlier slideshow was replaced by the gallery. Three photographs from the earlier slideshow (`field-jadukata-DJI_0066`, `field-jadukata-SYL_3185`, `field-someshwari-IMG_2901`) remain published as files but are no longer shown. The original field sources are unchanged.

All media come from the owner-provided Bangladesh Field Media Staging collection, recorded during field visits in January 2023. Every image and the video were visually checked before publication. The November selection reviewed 183 drone and ground photographs and the original drone videos from the same visits. Selected frames have no recognizable faces; people appear only as small distant figures.

`FIELD-PHOTOS.json` records every published photograph and the video: original camera filename, original SHA256, capture/visit date, visit area, archive manifest, and whether a photograph is currently shown. Dates use capture metadata and manifests where available. `20230106_133400.jpg` has no EXIF capture date, so its January 6 date is supported by its filename and the Bichanakandi visit manifest. Sunamganj / Jadukata labels identify the visit area rather than a precise river reach.

The video is seconds 4 to 16 of an original Someshwari drone clip recorded on 26 January 2023. It was downscaled to 1280×720 and re-encoded without sound; nothing else was changed. Edited or compiled videos, satellite imagery, and paper figures are not used.

Photographs and video document historical field conditions and do not establish present conditions, legal status, or measured landscape change. Captions describe what is visible.

Only resized JPEG derivatives and the re-encoded clip are published. EXIF/GPS and other source metadata are omitted. No objects or scene content were added or removed. Exact coordinates and local source paths are not published. Source files, filenames, and archive classifications remain unchanged.
