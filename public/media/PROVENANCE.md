# Field photograph provenance

The public gallery contains nine field photographs, with a separate Someshwari opening photograph. Five additional photographs replaced two videos and three Lubha paper figures on 25 October 2026. The replaced videos, posters, and paper figures were removed from the published assets. The original field sources are unchanged.

The ten selected photographs come from the owner-provided Bangladesh Field Media Staging collection. Images were visually checked before publication. The first selection reviewed 161 images; the replacement selection additionally sampled 96 ground photographs and revisited the drone collection. Selected frames have no recognizable faces.

`FIELD-PHOTOS.json` records every published photograph, original camera filename, original SHA256, capture/visit date, visit area, and archive manifest. Dates use capture metadata and manifests where available. `20230106_133400.jpg` has no EXIF capture date, so its January 6 date is supported by its filename and the Bichanakandi visit manifest. Sunamganj / Jadukata labels identify the visit area rather than a precise river reach.

Photographs document historical field conditions and do not establish present conditions, legal status, or measured landscape change. Captions describe what is visible.

Only resized JPEG derivatives are published. EXIF/GPS and other source metadata are omitted. No objects or scene content were added or removed. Exact coordinates and local source paths are not published. Source files, filenames, and archive classifications remain unchanged.
