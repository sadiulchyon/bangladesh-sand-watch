# Visual media provenance

These files were supplied through the site owner's Google Drive for Bangladesh Sand Watch (branch `visual-field-media-2026-10-15`). They are bundled locally so playback and figures do not depend on private Drive previews.

For the public site the images were resized (largest edge 2400 px, plus small thumbnails) and **all EXIF metadata was removed**. The original drone photograph carried GPS coordinates, a capture timestamp and camera details, which are not published. The videos were remuxed without metadata (no re-encoding) for faster streaming, and poster frames were taken at 1 s.
