01

A bounded sample

Eight bilingual searches returned 32 hits. Selection tested different source types and extraction difficulties. The 17 retained records are examples, not a complete national inventory.

02

Claims stay attributed

News allegations, editorial opinion, research findings, and official instruments remain distinct. A newspaper headline is not a legal determination. Accepting a record does not verify every claim.

03

Dates keep their context

Publication, update, first-online, notification, and posting dates are labeled. The consolidated 2010 law has no verified full date here. A project period is not a publication date.

04

Documents need checking

Only 4 of 35 pages of the 2025 rules and 2 of 4 pages of the Sunamganj tender were parsed. Bengali OCR contains errors. Deadlines, amounts, legal wording, and unread pages need visual review.

Current limits

This version contains a fixed pilot dataset. It does not collect new reports automatically. Nationwide recall, reviewer hours, and recurring operating cost have not been measured. Missing coverage does not show an absence of extraction. Summaries still require native-language editorial review.

Source registry & collection history

Planned sources are not automatically proof of coverage. Check dates refer to targeted searches, not every page on a website. Historical backfill and additional district portals will be added gradually.
